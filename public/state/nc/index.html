Federal money in North Carolina

In fiscal year 2025, federal agencies recorded $127 billion in grants, contracts and payments in North Carolina: $12,133 for each of its 10.4 million people, 100% of the national $12,154.

    Who paid federal income tax

    On 2023 tax returns, 4.9 million returns from North Carolina paid $52.1 billion in federal income tax, about $10,588 per return. Returns with $200,000 or more of income were 7.5% of the returns and paid 61.6% of the tax (nationally: 8.4% and 67.3%).
